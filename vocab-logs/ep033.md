# Vocabulario — Los primos de El Flaco

## Nuevo

- **la matrícula / la placa** — license plate (placa refreshed from day 16, matrícula new synonym)
- **el lavadero** — car wash
- **el parqueadero** — parking lot (Colombian term)
- **el registro** — official registry office
- **la comadre / el compadre** — co-mother/co-father: chosen kin through godparenthood, circled culturally
- **privado / privada** — private (vs. public/government)
- **la seguridad** — security (esp. private security firm)
- **el / la cliente** — client (refresh from day 11, now corporate sense)
- **averiguar** — to find out, investigate
- **la economía** — economy (formal vs. informal)
