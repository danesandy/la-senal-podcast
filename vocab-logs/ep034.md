# Vocabulario — Números primos

## Nuevo

- **el número primo** — prime number
- **contar** — to count / to tell (both senses explicitly circled)
- **el intervalo** — interval (refresh from day 10, now central)
- **la naranja** — orange (the fruit, used as math manipulative)
- **dividir** — to divide, split into equal parts
- **la fecha límite** — deadline
- **faltar** — to be lacking/remaining (faltan once días)
- **el amanecer** — dawn, sunrise
- **trasnochar** — to stay up all night
- **la pizarra** — whiteboard / blackboard
